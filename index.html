Why we act

It's our country. We see it through.

Nobody from another country is coming to fix our streets. Problems get solved when we take the initiative: report it, post it, back it up. This does not mean citizens paying out of pocket. It means people standing together and keeping it visible until the responsible officials act. Once people unite for a cause, no one can stop them.

Community rule

We fix problems, not point fingers.

Reports describe what is there, where it is and what needs to happen. We route to departments, never to individuals, so the focus stays on getting it fixed.

“That guy dumps garbage everywhere.”Not allowed. Names a person.
“Garbage pile at Lane 4. Needs pickup.”Good. Describes the problem and the place.
